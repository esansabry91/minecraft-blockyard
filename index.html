PLAYING TOGETHER

    BUILD HELPER

    Stand where you like and face the way you like. It appears right in front of you, made from the block you hold.

    PICK A BLOCK

    It goes into the slot you have selected. Try the fireworks, bounce pad, ice, animal egg, flowers and star lamp.

    WORKSHOP

    Collect parts, then put them together to make a weapon. Break logs for wood, break stone for stone, pick up pink magic gems or find treasure for gems, and swim in water for water drops.
